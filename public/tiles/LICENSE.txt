Basemap tiles in this directory
================================

Source data: Natural Earth (public domain)
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

  Australia — 1:10m vectors, rendered by scripts/prepare_offline_tiles_v2_for_deckgl.py:
    physical: land, bathymetry (200-6000m bands), lakes, lakes_australia, playas,
              rivers_lake_centerlines, rivers_australia
    cultural: urban_areas, admin_1_states_provinces_lines
    https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/
    https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/

  England — 1:50m "land" vectors only, rendered by
  scripts/prepare_offline_tiles_for_deckgl.py (v1, not re-rendered since).
    https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-physical-vectors/

No attribution required; safe for commercial use.

These PNG tiles are not distributed by Natural Earth directly — they were
rendered from Natural Earth's public-domain vector data by this project's
own scripts (above), and inherit the same public-domain status.

Note: the terms page (checked 2026-10-18) makes no exception for any theme
used here, Admin-1 state borders included. Its third-party data notes cover
river/lake data for Europe only — that doesn't apply to the Australian tiles.
